🛒 E-Commerce Pharmacy App Feature Set
1️⃣ Storefront (Patient / Customer Side)

✅ Product Catalog – Categories for medicines, supplements, medical devices, personal care, etc.
✅ Search & Filters – Search by name, category, brand, or health condition.
✅ Product Pages – Each product with:

Name, image, dosage, description

Price, availability (in stock / out of stock)

Add to cart / buy now
✅ Prescription Upload (if needed) – For prescription-only medicines, user must upload prescription before checkout.
✅ Shopping Cart & Wishlist – Users can save items for later.
✅ Checkout Process – Smooth, with:

Delivery address

Payment method (M-Pesa, card, wallet, etc.)

Order confirmation

2️⃣ Payments & Checkout

✅ Multiple Payment Methods

M-Pesa STK push (Paybill / Till number)

Card payments (Visa, Mastercard)

Bank transfer instructions
✅ Cash on Delivery (Optional)
✅ Discount Codes / Coupons
✅ VAT / Tax Calculation (if applicable)

3️⃣ Logistics & Delivery

✅ Delivery & Pickup Options

Home delivery with delivery fee

Pickup from hospital pharmacy
✅ Delivery Tracking – Status updates: Processing → Out for Delivery → Delivered
✅ Delivery Scheduling – Customer chooses delivery time window

4️⃣ User Accounts

✅ Sign Up / Login – Email, phone number, OTP verification
✅ Profile Management – Personal info, address, medical history
✅ Order History – Track past purchases
✅ Repeat Orders – Reorder previously purchased medicines
✅ Notifications – Push notifications for promotions, order updates

5️⃣ Marketing & Engagement

✅ Promotions & Banners – Highlight special offers, discounts
✅ Featured Products – Show best sellers or hospital-recommended items
✅ Loyalty Points / Rewards – Encourage repeat purchases
✅ Push Notifications – Remind users of abandoned carts, offer discounts

6️⃣ Admin & Pharmacy Dashboard

✅ Product Management – Add/edit/delete products, manage categories
✅ Inventory Management – Stock level alerts, auto-update when orders placed
✅ Order Management – View, approve, fulfill, cancel orders
✅ Prescription Verification Panel – For orders requiring doctor approval
✅ Reports & Analytics – Sales trends, popular products, revenue tracking
✅ User Management – Manage customers and pharmacy staff accounts

7️⃣ Security & Compliance

✅ GDPR/Local Data Compliance – Securely handle patient data
✅ Role-Based Access Control – Different permissions for admins, pharmacists, delivery staff
✅ Encrypted Payments – Secure checkout (SSL, PCI compliance)
✅ Audit Trail – Track all orders and prescription approvals

8️⃣ Technical Requirements

✅ Cross-Platform App – React Native (Android & iOS) + React Web App
✅ Scalable Backend – Node.js/Django + PostgreSQL/MySQL
✅ Hosting & Domain – Cloud-based, with hospital subdomain (e.g. pharmacy.lutonhospital.co.ke)
✅ CI/CD Pipeline – Smooth updates and bug fixes

💡 Pro Tip:
Launch with core e-commerce features first (catalog, cart, checkout, payment, delivery) and add advanced features (loyalty points, prescription verification, live chat) in later updates.
